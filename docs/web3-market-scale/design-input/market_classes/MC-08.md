# MC-08 — Cross-domain liquidity и inventory rebalancing

**PROPOSED_RND_NOT_RUN**, P2.

## Клиент и продукт

Intent solvers, bridge/liquidity operators, CEX–DEX treasury teams.

Read-only rebalancing planner с trapped inventory/timeout scenarios и quality/cost comparison.

Спрос/выручка UNKNOWN.

## Экономические преобразования

intent_fulfillment, bridge_claim, transfer, hedge, refund, reconcile

## Переиспользовать

- `src/inventory/non_atomic.py`
- `src/inventory/research.py`
- `src/mechanism_discovery/intent_graph.py`
- `src/mechanism_discovery/pr356_state_completion.py`

Связанные WG: WG-17, WG-18, WG-20. R&D parents: CLR-01, CLR-02.

## Три продукта

- Clearing: Сопоставлять встречные domain obligations, чтобы реже переносить inventory.
- Verified compute: Finality, settlement assumptions, refund/timeout and exact fee evidence.
- Mechanism lab: Compare inventory reservation, filler auctions и failure allocation policies.

## Первый эксперимент

2 domains, finite pre-funded balances,10 requests; compare transfer-every-time против netting/rebalance schedule с одной failed leg.

## Измерить

Capital by domain, completion delay, failed/unknown obligations, loss scenarios и transfer costs.

## Финансирование и время

Prefunding/credit и asynchronous settlement; interoperable order format не гарантирует atomicity.

## Ограничения и причины отказа

Bridge/security/finality assumptions, non-executable quote, domain-specific token identity, unavailable refund proof.

## Масштабирование

Customer intents и reuse resolver adapters; capacity constrained общим inventory и доступом к исполнению.
