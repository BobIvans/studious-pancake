# PR-353 qualification matrix

All nine packages remain `DISABLED` after merge. Their qualification functions
can produce at most `VERIFIED_SHADOW`.

Common rejection criteria include stale/unfinalized evidence, insufficient
replay, policy rejection, non-positive worst-case net after known costs,
unbounded tail risk, missing or non-transferable rights, future-data leakage,
clock/source-latency artefacts, unstable residual clusters, null-control failure
and family-wide multiple-testing failure.

Discovery data cannot qualify the same hypothesis. Holdout/walk-forward splits
and preregistration remain mandatory. Negative and inconclusive results are valid
research closure outcomes and do not create live permission.
