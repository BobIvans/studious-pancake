**PROPOSED_NOT_RUN · P2 · domain-specific market integrity**

## Экономическое условие

Parity only for matching conditions, collateral and exercisable merge rights; subtract lot dust and all fees.

## Первый ограниченный эксперимент

Synthetic 2-outcome complete set and 2 books: full set, wrong condition, partial acquisition, expired unclosed market, shared BalanceManager conflict.

## Остановить или сузить

Inactive deployment, stale oracle, incomplete book or missing subscription proof; separate outcomes conflated.

Market integrity / depth / completeness evidence API. Спрос и доходность UNKNOWN.
