# CB-05 — Cross-domain intents → inventory netting → settlement evidence

**PROPOSED_NOT_RUN · P2 · non-atomic inventory**

## Экономическое условие

Delivered-flow revenue minus gas, funding duration, rebalancing, failures and service cost; capacity constrained by capital until reimbursement.

## Первый ограниченный эксперимент

2 domains, 2 assets, 10 intents; compare one-way prefunding and two-way matched inventory. Stress proof delay, origin reorg, fill timeout, refund and duplicate events.

## Остановить или сузить

Claimed turnover uses fill time instead of settlement time; trapped inventory exceeds budget; unknown finality assumptions.

## Возможный продукт

Inventory/rebalancing planner и settlement reconciliation API. Спрос и доходность UNKNOWN.

## Совместимость

- No ordinary cross-chain flashloan assumption.
- Proof status, fill status and spendable balance are separate state machines.
