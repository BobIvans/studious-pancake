## Mission

PR-358 is one research-only integration over merged PR-357. It composes the existing AGG-02 market-data journal/source truth, AGG-10 relation baselines, PR-197/198 causal/Hawkes research, EVO-09 anomaly coverage, PR-357 lifecycle/world-model/VOI contracts, and AGG-14 evidence/product owners.

## Integrated vertical

The frozen in-repo synthetic fixture is journaled through canonical AGG-02 contracts, materialized into DB-01/DB-03/DB-05 PIT-safe views, evaluated through a deterministic content-addressed DAG, exact cache reuse and source-revision invalidation, control episodes, baseline/challenger relation methods and family-level FDR. A scoped predictive relation is compiled into proposal-only PR-357 hypothesis/world-model/bootstrap/VOI inputs and projected through the existing AGG-14 data-evidence policy into an entitlement-aware research service artifact.

The vertical never claims causal truth, executable arbitrage, live execution, realized PnL, customer billing, or actual machine payment.

## Data rights and privacy

Only the checked-in public synthetic fixture is ingested by this PR. External sources/tools remain references until their license/ToS/retention/redaction/redistribution/entitlement terms are reverified. Private orderflow, credentials, third-party bot surveillance and access-control bypass are forbidden.

## Relation method ladder

Simple deterministic controls and rank/lagged baselines remain the default. Directional-information and excitation challengers may be compared, but complexity is rejected when it does not add held-out value. Family-level trial accounting/FDR, negative controls, clock/common-cause checks, stability, sign/topology breaks and counterexamples are first-class evidence.

## Service/exchange laboratory

Market-science offers, evidence products, solver/keeper/sponsorship/x402/trust/proof/confidentiality surfaces are typed offline laboratories. Catalog records reference immutable evidence; pricing and unit economics are simulated only; `customer_billing=false`, `external_service_activation=false`, `execution_right=false`.

## Rollback

One PR revert. Derived cache/views/atlas/catalog projections may be rebuilt or dropped without deleting canonical evidence. No rollback path touches external services, capital, payments, signing, submission or release state.

> This PR merges research-only experiment, relation-science, and simulated service-economy components. It does not prove causal truth, executable arbitrage, profitability, production readiness, or live authorization.
