## Три продукта

- Clearing: Batch совместимых demands for resources; конфликтующие writes/deadlines не объединять механически.
- Verified compute: Calibrated inclusion/latency evidence и независимый receipt, не provider self-reported guarantee.
- Mechanism lab: Compare fee bid/deadline allocation и service guarantee models.

## Первый эксперимент

Recorded/synthetic fee regimes и timeout traces; compare static budget vs calibrated bounded choices на одинаковом deadline workload.

## Измерить

Missed deadlines, paid cost, calibration error, regime-change robustness и shared-capacity conflicts.
