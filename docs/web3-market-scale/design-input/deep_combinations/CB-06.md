# CB-06 — Funding claims ↔ perps / new builder markets

**PROPOSED_NOT_RUN · P2 · carry / hedge research**

## Экономическое условие

Matched cashflows after entry/exit fees, funding, basis, margin and failure losses; fixed quoted spread alone is insufficient.

## Первый ограниченный эксперимент

1 underlying, 2 perps, 2 rate claims; compare perfect synthetic hedge with mismatch in index, maturity, session and one failed fill. Baseline equal capital and duration.

## Остановить или сузить

Cannot prove payoff match; position liquidates in stress; no hedge during trading halt; credit/issuer risk ignored.

## Возможный продукт

Rate-hedge analytics и multi-venue exposure monitor. Спрос и доходность UNKNOWN.

## Совместимость

- No flash funding for held maturity exposure.
- Offchain/CEX and onchain legs remain non-atomic even behind one UI.
