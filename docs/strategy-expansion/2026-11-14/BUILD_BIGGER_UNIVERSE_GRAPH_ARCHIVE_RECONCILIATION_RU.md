## 3. What the first graph ZIP actually contributed

The initial input was not “add more token symbols.” It proposed an economic relationship graph:
- start from canonical AssetIdentity and its issuer/program/decimals/representation;
- distinguish underlying EconomicAsset from native/wrapped/bridged/LST/LRT/yield/LP representations;
- discover MarketIdentity/venue/pool/book and StructuralAnchor;
- form DIRECT, FRAGMENTATION, SYNTHETIC, STRUCTURAL, LST, NAV, STABLE, ORACLE, FLASH-CAPITAL and CROSS-CHAIN relation hypotheses;
- score local observation heat and schedule cheap data before expensive quote/state verification;
- preserve shared-liquidity aliases and source correlation groups;
- qualify only exact same-domain state/amount/quote/funding evidence before adding a directed edge to the executable graph.

The first 519 rows were explicitly UNRESOLVED_HYPOTHESES_NOT_EXECUTABLE; canonical A/B IDs and market identities were null. This archive is a research seed, not a pool registry.
